**DDEX1-07 Drums in the Marsh**

For the past several nights, drums have been heard on the wind, coming from the Twilight Marsh. Each morning, more turn up missing from the nearby farms, leaving only muddy, clawed footprints heading towards the marsh. Can you help find the lost and bring justice to the abductors?

_This is a Tier 1 adventure for character levels 1-4._
